# On Walking

Walking is the slowest honest way to cross a city. A walker sees the signs, the doors, the faces.[^1]

## The first mile

The first mile is the hardest. After it the body forgets its complaints and the mind starts to work.

> A man who walks does not hurry, and a man who hurries does not see.

Three habits help:

- Leave the phone in the pocket.
- Pick a street never walked before.
- Stop when something is interesting.

## The last mile

The last mile belongs to the evening. The *lights* come on, and **the city** changes its voice.

[^1]: This claim is old. Thoreau made it in 1862.
